The following are **not** facts, and the ledger doesn't cover them:
- decorative texture: DNS query strings on the reserved `example.net` domain, feed tiles, network dots, notice paper, signature strokes, identicons, the swarm field
- generic illustrations: the satellite and aerial views are not real imagery of named sites
- lyric text
- counts drawn as squares or strokes, which are illustrative

## Rules
- Use the source's precision: `~` for estimates, ranges as ranges, and no invented digits. Bars are drawn to scale.
- **Reported** items (one outlet, anonymous sources, or estimates) carry `REPORTED` or `ESTIMATE`, or are attributed in the caption.
- Quotation marks are only for exact words, 15 words or fewer, attributed. Anything else is labelled paraphrase or set without quotes.
- No p(doom) counter. p(doom) percentages appear only where the lyric invokes them: Hubinger's ">10%" (V3.5), and the published estimates on "who can tell?" (static, each with its own wording and source, never summed).
- Real harm (Grok's images, the FSU shooting, the lawsuits, the Iran campaign) is shown cold: no jokes, no victims, no chat content, no names beyond public officials and case numbers.
