# Chorus ×4 + Final Chorus extras: line research (THE RECORD)

Researched 2026-09-28. **Verified** means a primary document or 2+ independent outlets back it. A company's own filing, policy, system card or report counts as primary for its own contents. **Reported** means one outlet, one analyst, or estimates (Epoch's satellite-based figures count as estimates).

**Slots.** Ch1 = *finish*, Ch2 = *deadline*, Ch3 = *red line*, Final = *cliff edge*. Lines 1, 2, 3 and the p(doom) hook appear 4 times. **"China's just months off" appears only 3 times**, because the final chorus swaps it for "If this is the finish, who's left behind?". The final-only lines (6a–6c) get one slot each, so their four frames are alternates.

## Discrepancies with `annotations.md`

1. **Capex is stale.** "Big Four ≈ $630B" was the **February** guidance. After the July calls the four companies alone guide **$720–745B** for 2026: Amazon ~$220B, Alphabet $195–205B, Meta $130–145B, Microsoft ~$175B (calendar 2026, after a lease-accounting change). "Seven hundred billion" is now the Big Four alone, not only the broader trackers. The Feb sum also used ~$110–120B for Microsoft; Microsoft's own calendar-2026 figure (~$190B, April) was far higher.
2. **DeepSeek "3 to 6 months" is DeepSeek's own estimate**, from its V4 technical report, not an analyst's. Epoch's independent figure is **~4 months** (open vs closed models, Jan–May 2026).
3. **"Built to train and run on Chinese chips" goes too far.** It is verified that V4 runs on Huawei Ascend (day-zero support, and the tech report validates it on NVIDIA GPUs *and* Ascend NPUs). The training hardware wasn't disclosed and is disputed.
4. **"About 20% among prominent public voices"** (Final Chorus p(doom) row) isn't in either cited source. Neither Gizmodo nor Wikipedia has it, so it's unsourced. Gizmodo also notes Hubinger earlier put existential risk at "around 80%". The >10% figure is his within-a-decade number.
5. **"Six weeks after it was disclosed."** Hugging Face disclosed the breach **Jul 16**, and the Nvidia deal came **Sep 3**, 7 weeks later. It is ~6 weeks only if you count from the Jul 21 joint statement that named OpenAI's agents.
6. **"AI is the top stated reason for layoffs"** is true **year-to-date** (116,175, ~22% through Aug). In August, though, AI fell to #4. The "35% since 2023" figure is **Revelio Labs** (since Jan 2023). The annotation cites a secondary site.
7. **MIRI's wording** is "than we have in a long time", not "in years".

## 2. "Pause ripped out, the finish / deadline / red line / cliff edge is nearing"

**Context.**

The intro called the RSP a commitment not to train or deploy without adequate safeguards.
- **RSP v3.0, effective Feb 24, 2026 (19 pp.)**, is a "comprehensive rewrite". It splits company plans from "industry-wide recommendations" it "cannot commit to following … unilaterally", and replaces the pause with Frontier Safety Roadmaps (non-binding goals) and Risk Reports every 3–6 months.
- v3.0 Appendix A keeps a **conditional** delay: Anthropic delays only if it is in the lead, or to match competitors that have strong safety measures. Under "General upleveling" it will "not necessarily delay".
- Jared Kaplan to TIME (Feb 24): unilateral commitments didn't make sense "if competitors are blazing ahead". The Board approved it unanimously.
- Timelines:
  - Oct 28, 2025 livestream: OpenAI set an internal goal of an automated AI research intern by Sep 2026 and an automated AI researcher by **Mar 2028**. On ~Sep 7, 2026 it said the intern goal was met on schedule.
  - AI Futures Project, Kokotajlo's automated-coder median: late 2029, then **mid 2028** (Apr 2 update), then **Nov 2027** (Aug 16). Lifland moved from mid 2030 to Jan 2030.

**Context.**
