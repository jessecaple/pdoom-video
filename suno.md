**Styles**

```
Chiptune-heavy chaotic hyperpop, a loud glitchy 8-bit chip lead melody running through every section, with digicore, glitchcore and rage edges, 152 BPM, F major, dirty and maximal. Busy bitcrushed chip arpeggios under the verses, exploding into blown-out, clipping, distorted 808 drops; dark, bass-dominant, overdriven mix with gritty noise. Hard-tuned robotic autotune lead vocal pushed into distortion and saturation, high register, pitch-warped and bitcrushed in places, sneering fast emo sing-rap verses, pitched-up vocal chops, screamed ad-libs. Off-kilter, detuned, warbling synth melodies, rage-style leads, stutter edits, sudden beat switches, noise bursts, half-time bridge, a cappella break, abrupt ending. Manic, anxious, unhinged.
```

**Exclude styles**

```
acoustic, folk, country, orchestral, jazz, reggae, metal, heavy guitars, slow ballad, clean polished pop, bright radio pop, pristine vocals, smooth vocals, soft vocals, acoustic drums, heavy vocal reverb, nightcore, spoken word, mumbled vocals
```
