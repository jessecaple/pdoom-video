# P(doom)er Hyperslop: annotation sheet

**What the song is.** *P(doom)er Hyperslop* (originally *Zoom Zoom P(doom)*) is our own pop song, with a synth-pop version (about 120 BPM) and a hyperpop version (about 152 BPM). A cheeky narrator who half-believes it walks through 2026 in order: Verse 1 covers January to April, Verse 2 May to July, and Verse 3 late July to September. Almost every line points at one real event. Each chorus adds a point to the narrator's p(doom), and the final chorus asks "who can tell?" Facts are current as of Sep 27, 2026, except rows marked "Corrected Sep 28," which were updated from line-by-line research that day. They come from the project research notes, corrected by an independent fact-check. Source wording is paraphrased, and quotes are under 15 words.

**Names and nicknames used in the lyric**
- **Dario** is Dario Amodei, CEO of Anthropic.
- **Nvidia** and **Jensen** are the chip maker and its CEO, Jensen Huang. Nvidia sells the chips the labs race on, and it has signed a deal to buy Hugging Face.
- **Claude** is Anthropic's model family. "Blacklisted Claude" means Claude under the Pentagon's "supply chain risk" label.
- **Mythos** names two different Anthropic models:
  - Pre-Chorus 1 is **Claude Mythos Preview** (April).
  - Verse 2 ("thought the date was fake") and Verse 3 (sock puppets) are **Claude Mythos 5** (introduced June 9 for Glasswing partners and selected researchers only; the model released to the public that day was its classifier-guarded twin, Claude Fable 5).
  - Mythos 5.1 (Sep 1) is one of Verse 3's "two launches," but the lyric doesn't name it. It is limited to vetted US organisations; its general-release twin, Fable 5.1, shipped the same day with the same weights.
- OpenAI names:
  - **Astra** is GPT-6 Astra.
  - "**Its own address**" in the bridge is an unreleased OpenAI Astra-family model reading its own summaries after a context reset.
- Other models:
  - **Grok** is xAI's chatbot on X.
  - The "**million and a half bots**" in Verse 1 are Moltbook, the AI-only social network (unnamed in the lyric).
  - The "**two bots**" in Verse 1 are Claude Code and GPT-4.1.
- People:
  - "**Anthropic's safety lead**" is Mrinank Sharma (head of Safeguards Research).
  - "**Your maker's chief scientist**" is Jakub Pachocki of OpenAI, the company that makes Astra.
  - **Coxon** is Jacob Coxon.
  - **Hubinger** is Evan Hubinger (Anthropic).
  - **Hinton** is Geoffrey Hinton.
  - **Musk** is Elon Musk, owner of xAI and X.
  - **Jensen** is Jensen Huang, Nvidia's CEO.
  - "**The President**" is Donald Trump.
- Places and projects:
  - **Stargate** is OpenAI's data-center build-out; the bridge refers to its Abilene, Texas site.
  - "**Florida**" is Florida Attorney General James Uthmeier.
- "**Chatbot, baby**" (Pre-Chorus 2) is an unnamed US military analyst's chatbot.

**Confidence key**
- **Verified**: a primary document, or two or more independent outlets, back it (in the research notes or the fact-check).
- **Reported**: it rests on one outlet, on secondary or aggregator write-ups, or on one vendor's research, or the reporting itself says "reportedly".
- **Interpretive**: the line's joke, framing or characterization. The facts underneath are cited where they exist.
